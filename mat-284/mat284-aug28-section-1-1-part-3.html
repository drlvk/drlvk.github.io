ToolPercentages

Read the percent sign as “divided by 100.” That single reading handles every percentage problem in this course.

So 35% of a quantity \(x\) is

\[\frac{35}{100}\cdot x\]

For instance, 35% of 40 is \(\frac{35}{100}\cdot 40 = 14\).

Worth memorizing

  • \(x\) increased by 35% is \(\;x + \frac{35}{100}x = \left(1 + \frac{35}{100}\right)x = 1.35x\)
  • \(x\) reduced by 35% is \(\;x - \frac{35}{100}x = \left(1 - \frac{35}{100}\right)x = 0.65x\)

Factoring out the \(x\) is what turns a two-term expression into a single multiplier. Once you have \(1.35x\) or \(0.65x\), the rest of the problem is ordinary algebra.

Worked exampleProfit as a percent of price

Careful with this one. It is very easy to get wrong, because the percentage is taken of a quantity you do not know yet.

Each unit costs you $200 to make. You want the profit to be 50% of the selling price. What should the price be?

Show solution
  1. Let\(x =\) the selling price of one unit, in dollars
  2. Profit\[\text{profit} = x - 200\]
  3. Goal\[\text{profit} = \frac{50}{100}\cdot \text{price}\]
  4. Equation\[x - 200 = \frac{50}{100}\cdot x = 0.5x\]
  5. Collect\[(1 - 0.5)x = 200\]
  6. Simplify\[0.5x = 200\]
  7. Solve\[x = \frac{200}{0.5} = 400\]

Answer$400 per unit

Why this one is a trap

The 50% is 50% of the price, and the price is the unknown. So the percentage lands on the same letter you are solving for, and both occurrences of \(x\) have to be collected on one side. The wrong reading is to take 50% of the cost, which gives a $300 price and a profit of only $100.

Check: at a price of $400 the profit is \(400 - 200 = 200\), which is indeed half of $400.

Worked exampleWhen not every unit sells

Your company has fixed cost $90,000, variable cost $8 per unit, and a selling price of $20. But not every unit sells: 10% of the product ends up expired, damaged, or stolen.

How many units must be produced so that the profit is at least $25,000?

Show solution

The plan for any problem of this kind: state the business relations, then fill them in with formulas.

  1. Goal“at least” is non-strict: \[\text{profit} \ge 25000\]
  2. Needa formula for profit \(=\) revenue \(-\) (total cost)
  3. Let\(x =\) the number of units made
  4. Total costevery unit made costs money: \[\text{total cost} = 90000 + 8x\]
  5. Quantity soldonly 90% of them sell: \(\left(1 - \frac{10}{100}\right)x = 0.9x\)
  6. Revenue\[\text{revenue} = 20 \cdot 0.9x = 18x\]
  7. Profit\[\text{profit} = 18x - (90000 + 8x) = 10x - 90000\]
  8. Inequality\[10x - 90000 \ge 25000\]
  9. Solve\[10x \ge 115000 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x \ge 11500\]

Answer11,500 units produced

Which formula the 10% touches

Spoilage changes the revenue formula and leaves the cost formula alone. You paid to make all \(x\) units; you only get paid for \(0.9x\) of them. Applying the 10% to the cost as well is the standard way to lose this problem.

Note also what the answer counts. \(x\) was defined as units made, so 11,500 is units produced, not units sold.

Worked exampleBase salary plus commission

This one looks like a percentage problem, but more is going on. Careful reading is what makes the translation accurate.

An employee of a computer store is paid a base salary of $2,000 a month plus an 8% commission on all sales over $7,000 during the month. How much must the employee sell in one month to earn a total of $4,000?

This was the Poll Everywhere question.

Show solution

Let \(x\) be the sales volume in dollars. We want to find it from the relation monthly income \(= 4000\). The base salary alone is not enough, so there has to be some commission income, which means \(x > 7000\).

The key step is expressing the income in terms of \(x\). The commission is 8% of sales over $7,000, so the quantity being multiplied is \(x - 7000\).

  1. Commission\[\frac{8}{100}\cdot(x - 7000) = 0.08(x - 7000)\]
  2. Income\[\text{income} = 2000 + 0.08(x - 7000)\]
  3. Equation\[2000 + 0.08(x - 7000) = 4000\]
  4. Subtract 2000\[0.08(x - 7000) = 2000\]
  5. Divide by 0.08\[x - 7000 = \frac{2000}{0.08} = 25000\]
  6. Add 7000\[x = 32000\]

Answer$32,000 in sales

Check: sales above $7,000 come to $25,000, and 8% of that is $2,000. Base plus commission is $4,000, and \(32000 > 7000\) as required.

Watch out

The commission is not 8% of sales. Writing \(0.08x\) instead of \(0.08(x - 7000)\) gives $25,000, which is wrong. The phrase “on all sales over $7,000” is doing real work in that sentence.

Notice too that dividing by \(0.08\) before expanding the parenthesis saves a line of algebra.

MethodAllocation problems

Sometimes the job is to decide how to split a given amount between two options. These all follow one recipe.

How to approach such problems

  1. Introduce two variables, one for each option.
  2. Translate the statement into an equation or inequality with these variables.
  3. Find a relationship between the two variables and use it to eliminate one of them.
  4. Solve the equation or inequality for the remaining variable.

A company produces coffee makers. The labor cost of assembling one coffee maker during regular business hours is $2.55. If the work is done in overtime, the labor cost is $4.55 per unit.

The company must produce 580 coffee makers this week, and does not want to spend more than $1,719 in labor costs. What is the smallest number of units that must be assembled during the regular hours?

Show solution
  1. Two variables\(x =\) units made in regular time, \(y =\) units made in overtime
  2. Labor cost\[2.55x + 4.55y\]
  3. Want“not more than” is non-strict: \[2.55x + 4.55y \le 1719\]
  4. Relation\[x + y = 580 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y = 580 - x\]
  5. Substitute\[2.55x + 4.55(580 - x) \le 1719\]
  6. Expand\[2.55x + 2639 - 4.55x \le 1719\]
  7. Collect\[-2x \le -920\]
  8. Divide by −2the sign flips: \[x \ge 460\]

Answer460 units in regular hours

Check: 460 regular units and 120 overtime units cost \(460(2.55) + 120(4.55) = 1173 + 546 = 1719\), exactly the limit.

Two things to notice

Dividing both sides by a negative number flips the inequality. This is where most of the lost points on this problem come from.

The answer is a floor rather than a ceiling, and that is the right shape. More regular hours means less spending, so the budget forces \(x\) to be large, not small. If your answer had come out as \(x \le 460\), the sign flip went missing.

Worked exampleSplitting an investment

This one has percentages and a split between two options, so both tools from today appear at once.

Suppose you have $500,000 to invest. Fund A pays 5.2% annually; Fund B pays 7.7% annually, though it is riskier. How should you divide your money between Fund A and Fund B to produce an annual interest income of $34,000?

Show solution
  1. Two variables\(x =\) money in Fund A, \(y =\) money in Fund B, both in dollars
  2. Want\[\frac{5.2}{100}x + \frac{7.7}{100}y = 34000\]
  3. Relation\[x + y = 500000 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y = 500000 - x\]
  4. Substitute\[0.052x + 0.077(500000 - x) = 34000\]
  5. Expand\[0.052x + 38500 - 0.077x = 34000\]
  6. Collect\[-0.025x = -4500\]
  7. Solve\[x = \frac{-4500}{-0.025} = 180000\]
  8. Find y\[y = 500000 - 180000 = 320000\]

Answer$180,000 in Fund A and $320,000 in Fund B

Check: \(0.052(180000) = 9360\) and \(0.077(320000) = 24640\), and \(9360 + 24640 = 34000\). ✓

Finish the job

After you solve for \(x\), go back and find \(y\) as well. The question asks how to divide the money, so a complete answer names both amounts. Stopping at \(x = 180000\) answers half the question.

A quick sanity check on the answer: 5.2% of the whole $500,000 would be $26,000 and 7.7% would be $38,500. The target of $34,000 sits between them, closer to the top, so most of the money should be in Fund B. It is.

Worked exampleConcert tickets

What type of problem is this?

A concert brought in $129,000 from the sale of 3,800 tickets. If the tickets sold for $30 and $40 each, how many of each type of ticket were sold?

Show solution

It is an allocation problem. Nothing about tickets makes it different from the coffee makers or the two funds: a fixed total is split between two options with different unit values.

  1. Two variables\(x =\) tickets sold at $30, \(y =\) tickets sold at $40
  2. Total sales\[30x + 40y = 129000\]
  3. Relation\[x + y = 3800 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y = 3800 - x\]
  4. Substitute\[30x + 40(3800 - x) = 129000\]
  5. Expand\[30x + 152000 - 40x = 129000\]
  6. Collect\[-10x = -23000\]
  7. Solve\[x = 2300\]
  8. Find y\[y = 3800 - 2300 = 1500\]

Answer2,300 tickets at $30 and 1,500 at $40

Check: \(2300 + 1500 = 3800\) tickets, and \(30(2300) + 40(1500) = 69000 + 60000 = 129000\). ✓

The pattern behind the last three

  • Coffee makers: 580 units split between $2.55 and $4.55 labor
  • Investment: $500,000 split between 5.2% and 7.7% returns
  • Tickets: 3,800 tickets split between $30 and $40 prices

One fixed total, two options, two different rates. Name both parts, write the rate equation, use the total to eliminate one variable.
